## About This Book

*Wok Thinking* is a culinary intelligence guide for the people who operate at the intersection of food, systems and business. It is not a cookbook. It is not a business book. It is a practitioner's intelligence manual written by someone who has worked inside both the kitchen and the boardroom across fifteen APAC markets.
